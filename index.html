Most design goes wrong long before anyone opens the software. It goes wrong in what was agreed. This is how I run a project so that it does not happen to yours.

How a project runs

  1. 01

    Scope

    We agree what you are actually buying. A logo is one artifact. A brand identity is the system that logo lives inside: the type, the colour, the rules, the way it behaves on a poster and on a phone. A campaign identity is that system built to move quickly and hold together across a season. You leave this stage with a written list of what you get, so that nothing later has to be asked for as a favour.

  2. 02

    Discover

    Before anything is drawn I need to know what the brand has to do. Who it speaks to, what it is measured against, and what it has to survive: a launch, a season, a campaign with a fixed date. This stage is short, and it is the whole difference between design that decorates and design that works.

  3. 03

    Direction

    You see routes, not a single take it or leave it option. We choose one together and I take it to finished. This is where the shaping happens, so it is where the revisions belong.

    Includes two rounds of revision

  4. 04

    Handover

    The chosen direction is built out across everything it needs to appear on. You get the files, organised, in the formats you will actually use, and the guidance to keep the system intact once other people start working with it.

    Includes one round of revision

That is the method. This is what it produces.
