Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 July 2026

Draft for review. This document is a structurally complete starting point, not legal advice. Have a Kenyan advocate review it, particularly for the Data Protection Act, 2019 and whether Control Tee must register with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), before relying on it.

Who we are

Control Tee is a design studio based in Nairobi, Kenya. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you send an inquiry through our contact form, we receive your name, email address, company (if provided) and the contents of your message.
  • Booking information. If you book a call, the appointment is handled by Google Calendar's appointment scheduling, and the details you enter there are processed by Google.
  • Technical information. Our host records standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell personal information.

Why we use it

We use what you send us to reply to your inquiry, to arrange and deliver work, and to keep records of our business dealings. We rely on your consent when you contact us, and on our legitimate interest in running the studio.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf: our website host, our database and file storage provider, our form-delivery provider, and Google for appointment scheduling. We share information with them only so they can provide those services.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries and project correspondence for as long as we need them for the project and for our business records, then delete them.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to how we use it, and to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

To make a request, contact us through our contact page.

Client work and portfolio

We show work we have produced for clients on this website. If you are a client and would like a project removed, contact us and we will take it down.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
