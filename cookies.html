Cookie Policy

Last updated 3 August 2026

The short version

This site sets no advertising cookies and uses no third party analytics product. We do count page views in our own database, which needs no cookie and records nothing that identifies you: the address of the page, the site you arrived from, and the time. We do not track you across other websites, and we could not build a profile of you from what we store even if we wanted to. What is kept in your browser is limited to what makes the site work and remember your preferences.

Strictly speaking, these are not cookies

Everything below is stored using your browser's local and session storage rather than cookies. The practical difference is that none of it is sent to our server with each request. It stays in your browser. We call this a cookie policy because that is the phrase people search for.

What we store

  • Theme preference. When you switch between light and dark mode, we remember your choice in local storage so the site looks the same next time you visit.
  • Menu state and a brief cache. We remember which project categories you expanded, and cache the project list for a few minutes, in session storage. This clears when you close the tab.
  • This notice. We record that you dismissed the cookie notice so it does not reappear on every page.

All of it is strictly functional. Nothing is sent to advertisers, and none of it identifies you personally. Because none of this is used for tracking, we show you a notice rather than a consent gate. There is nothing here to withhold pending your permission.

Third parties

If you book a call, the booking page is provided by Google and opens on Google's own site, where Google's privacy and cookie policies apply. Nothing from Google is embedded in this site, and this site embeds no third party trackers of any kind.

Managing it

You can clear this site's stored data at any time through your browser settings, and you can browse with storage blocked entirely. The site will keep working either way. It will simply forget your theme choice and menu state, and fetch the project list fresh each time.
