Cookie Policy

Last updated 19 July 2026

Draft for review. This document is a structurally complete starting point, not legal advice. Have a Kenyan advocate review it, particularly for the Data Protection Act, 2019 and whether Control Tee must register with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), before relying on it.

The short version

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. We do not track you across other websites. What we store is limited to what makes the site work and remember your preferences.

What we store

  • Theme preference. When you switch between light and dark mode, we remember your choice in your browser's local storage so the site looks the same next time.
  • Menu state and a short-lived cache. We remember which project categories you expanded, and briefly cache the project list, in your browser's session storage. This clears when you close the tab.
  • This notice. We record that you dismissed the cookie notice so it does not reappear.

These are strictly functional: nothing is sent to advertisers, and none of it identifies you personally.

Third parties

If you book a call, the scheduling window is provided by Google and Google may set its own cookies inside it. That is covered by Google's own privacy and cookie policies. The rest of the site does not embed third-party trackers.

Managing it

You can clear this site's stored data at any time through your browser settings. The site will keep working; it will simply forget your theme choice and menu state.
